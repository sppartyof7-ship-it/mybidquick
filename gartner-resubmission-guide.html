Getting 3 Reviews on Gartner CRITICAL

This is the single biggest factor for approval. Gartner wants verified reviews from real users submitted through their platform. Here's exactly how to get reviews from your three people.

How Gartner Reviews Work

Reviews are submitted at gartner.com/reviews. The reviewer needs a business email (not Gmail/Yahoo), fills out a 10-15 minute survey, verifies their email, and the review enters a 3-5 business day moderation period. Gartner verifies the reviewer is a real professional.

Product Screenshots Needed TO DO

Gartner's listing needs real product screenshots. Take these from the live site:

  • Quote results with tiers — Show Standard/Premium/Platinum options (final prices only, no formulas!)
  • Tenant dashboard — Show the main dashboard with leads/credits overview
  • Kanban CRM — Show the drag-and-drop lead pipeline
  • Mobile view — Screenshot the quote page on a phone (or use Chrome DevTools mobile view)

How to Take Good Screenshots

Open Chrome, go to the page you want to screenshot
Press F12 to open DevTools, then Ctrl+Shift+P and type "screenshot"
Select "Capture full size screenshot" or "Capture area screenshot"
For mobile: click the phone icon in DevTools toolbar, pick "iPhone 14 Pro" or similar, then screenshot
Save as PNG — aim for at least 1280px wide for desktop shots
