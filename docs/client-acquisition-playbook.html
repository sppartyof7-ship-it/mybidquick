Cold Outreach Templates (DM + Email)

Your current cold outreach hasn't converted. These templates are built for solo pressure washers — they lead with pain, not features. The hook is always "try it free, zero risk."

Why cold outreach fails: Most SaaS cold DMs sound like "Hey, I built a tool, check it out!" Solo operators delete those instantly. Instead, call out something specific about THEIR business — their Google listing, their Facebook page, their website. Make it about them.

Instagram/Facebook DM — The "Saw Your Work" Opener

Use when: You find a pressure washer posting before/after photos on social media. Works best because it's genuine and specific.
Hey [first name] — your [driveway/deck/house] wash came out clean. Quick question: when a homeowner hits you up for a quote, how are you sending it back? Text? Phone call? I'm a pressure washer too and I got tired of going back and forth, so I built a tool that lets my customers get an instant quote from my website — no phone tag. It's called MyBidQuick. If you want to try it I'll set you up with 3 free quotes, no card needed. Takes about 5 minutes. Either way, solid work man. 🤙

Hey [first name] — I saw your pressure washing page. Do you have a website yet or are you running everything through Facebook? I ask because I built something that gives you a branded quote page ([their-name].mybidquick.com) where customers can get an instant price. No website needed. Your Weekly Plan (Under 2 Hours)

Here's how to spend your time each week. Do this every week for 8 weeks and you'll have paying customers.

Monday Send 5 cold DMs to pressure washers you found over the weekend. Use templates #1 or #2. 30 min
Wednesday Post 1 value post in a Facebook group (rotate groups weekly). Engage on 3-4 other people's posts with helpful comments. 30 min
Friday Follow up on any DMs from Monday (use template #4). Search Instagram/Facebook for 10 new targets for next Monday. 30 min
Weekend Browse PW groups casually — comment on quoting/pricing threads (use template #5 as a reply). Save good targets. 15 min
The math: 5 DMs/week × 8 weeks = 40 pressure washers contacted. At a 10% conversion rate (conservative for a free trial), that's 4 new tenants. At 25% of those buying a Growth pack ($50), that's $200/month recurring. The directory listings add passive inbound on top of that.
