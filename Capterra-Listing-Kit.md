```
MyBidQuick is an instant quoting platform built specifically for cleaning and exterior service companies — pressure washing, window cleaning, house washing, gutter cleaning, and more.

Your customers visit your branded quote page, select their services, answer a few quick questions about their property, and get an instant price. No phone tag. No waiting. No "I'll get back to you" — they see a professional quote in under 60 seconds.

BUILT BY A CLEANING COMPANY OWNER
MyBidQuick was created by a cleaning business owner who got tired of losing jobs because quotes took too long. Every feature is designed around how real cleaning companies actually operate — from smart service upsells to tiered pricing packages (Standard, Premium, Platinum).

WHITE-LABEL BRANDING
Each company gets their own branded subdomain (yourcompany.mybidquick.com) with their logo, colors, and services. Your customers never see "MyBidQuick" — it looks and feels like your own website.

SMART UPSELL ENGINE
When a customer requests a house wash quote, MyBidQuick automatically suggests windows and gutters. This cascade upsell system increases average ticket size by 20-35% without any extra effort from you.

BUILT-IN LEAD MANAGEMENT
Every quote submission flows into your dashboard with a drag-and-drop Kanban board for tracking leads through your pipeline — from new lead to booked job. Automated follow-up emails go out on Day 1, Day 3, and Day 7 to close more deals.

PAY PER LEAD PRICING
No monthly subscriptions. No contracts. Buy credit packs and only pay when a customer submits a quote. Start with 3 free credits — no credit card required.

Credit Packs:
- Starter: 10 credits / $25
- Growth: 25 credits / $50
- Pro: 50 credits / $85
- Agency: 100 credits / $150

EASY SETUP
Get your branded quote page live in under 10 minutes. Embed it on your website, share a direct link, or use an auto-generated QR code for yard signs and flyers.

PERFECT FOR:
- Pressure washing companies
- Window cleaning businesses
- House wash / soft wash companies
- Gutter cleaning services
```
